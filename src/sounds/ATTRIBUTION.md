# Dice sounds

The goal asked for the dice sounds of the Obsidian Dice Roller plugin
(github.com/javalent/dice-roller, MIT). That plugin ships no sounds: its
repository has no audio files and its renderer plays none (checked 29 September
2026, at the head of `main`). So, as the goal allowed, these are the sound
assets of **@3d-dice/dice-box-threejs** (v0.0.12,
github.com/3d-dice/dice-box-threejs), its "wood" set, copied unchanged from the
package's `public/sounds/`:

- `dicehit_wood1.mp3` … `dicehit_wood12.mp3` — a die striking a die
- `surface_wood_tray1.mp3` … `surface_wood_tray7.mp3` — a die striking the tray
- `surface_wood_table1.mp3` … `surface_wood_table7.mp3` — a die coming to rest

dice-box-threejs is a fork of Major's 3D Dice, itself built on Teall Dice.
